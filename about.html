About North Star Bakery

Our Story

North Star Bakery began in 2011 at a folding table at the Riverton farmers market, where founder Elena Marsh sold twelve loaves of sourdough made from her grandmother’s starter. Neighbors kept coming back, and within two years the bakery moved into its permanent home on Maple Street.

The name comes from a simple idea: a neighborhood bakery should be a steady place people can always find their way back to. More than a decade later, we still shape every loaf by hand and still know many of our customers by name.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

Good bread starts with good ingredients and good neighbors. We buy as close to home as we can and keep our recipes simple.

  • Local Flour

    Stone-ground flour from Miller Creek Mill, 30 miles from our ovens.

  • Farm Eggs and Dairy

    Delivered twice a week from family farms in the county.

  • Seasonal Fruit

    Our scones and tarts change with what local growers are harvesting.

  • Nothing Wasted

    No preservatives or artificial colors, and unsold bread is donated each evening to the Riverton Community Pantry.
