Welcome to North Star Bakery

Your Neighborhood Bakery

Good morning, neighbor! At North Star Bakery, every loaf, pastry, and cake is shaped by hand and baked fresh before the sun comes up. Whether you are grabbing breakfast on your way to the train, picking up bread for family dinner, or planning dessert for a celebration, we are glad you stopped by.

What We’re Known For

  • Slow-fermented breads: sourdough and whole grain loaves that rest for 24 hours before baking.
  • Buttery morning pastries: croissants, scones, and cinnamon rolls ready when the doors open at 6:30 a.m.
  • Custom celebration cakes: made to order for birthdays, weddings, and community events.
  • Easy pre-orders: reserve your favorites ahead of time and skip the morning line.
