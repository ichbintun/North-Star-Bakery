Our Products

Everything on our shelves is baked in small batches each morning. Prices are listed as ranges because sizes and seasonal ingredients vary. We do not offer online checkout, but you can send a pre-order request and pay when you pick up.

Breads

Price range: $6 to $12 per loaf

North Star Signature Loaf

Our best-known bread. A naturally leavened sourdough with a crisp crust and a soft, tangy crumb. $10 per loaf.

Honey Oat Harvest Loaf

Soft whole wheat bread sweetened with local honey and rolled in toasted oats. Great for school lunches. $8 per loaf.

Seeded Multigrain

A hearty loaf packed with sunflower, flax, and pumpkin seeds. $9 per loaf.

Classic Baguette

Baked twice a day so it is always fresh for dinner. $6 each.

Pastries

Price range: $3 to $6 each

Butter Croissant

Flaky, layered, and made with real butter over three days. $4 each.

Cinnamon Morning Roll

A soft spiral roll with brown sugar, cinnamon, and a light vanilla glaze. $5 each.

Seasonal Fruit Scone

Tender scones made with fruit from nearby farms. Flavors change with the season. $3.50 each.

Commuter Breakfast Box

Any two pastries boxed and ready to go for the morning train. $6 to $9 per box.

Cakes

Price range: $30 to $180, depending on size and decoration

Celebration Layer Cake

Vanilla, chocolate, or lemon layers with buttercream frosting. Serves 8 to 12 people. $45 to $70.

Family Sheet Cake

A simple, generous cake for parties and school events. Serves 20 to 40 people. $55 to $95.

Custom Event Cake

Tiered and decorated cakes for weddings and large gatherings. Please request at least two weeks ahead. $120 to $180.

Mini Cake for Two

A small four-inch cake for quiet celebrations. $30.

How to Order

  1. Choose your items from the categories above.
  2. Fill out the pre-order request form with your pickup date.
  3. We will email you to confirm your order and the final price.
  4. Pick up and pay at the bakery counter.
